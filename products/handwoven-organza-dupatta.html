Handwoven Gold Organza Dupatta

An heirloom finishing layer for solid kurtis, shararas, and ghagras, the Handwoven Gold Organza Dupatta is loomed in sheer silk organza with delicate floral butis, scalloped zari edges, and hand-knotted silk tassels.

Inquire for Price & Shades (WhatsApp)
Fabric & Craftsmanship

100% pure Banarasi silk organza handwoven with antique gold zari motifs and scalloped lace finishing along all four sides.

Dimensions & Drape

Free size: Length 2.50 meters | Width 1.05 meters. Featherlight crisp body that holds structured pleats or an open shoulder drape effortlessly.

Care Instructions

Dry clean only. Wrap in soft tissue paper before storing flat to prevent creasing.
