Casual / Kurta Set

Indigo Block-Print Cotton Kurta Set

Hand-stamped by master block printers in Jaipur, this pure cambric cotton kurta set blends deep natural indigo hues with crisp ivory botanical motifs. Paired with relaxed tapered pants featuring coordinated border trims for effortless daily dressing.

Inquire for Price & Sizes (WhatsApp)
Fabric & Craftsmanship

100% pure 60s cambric cotton dyed in natural indigo and hand-stamped using carved wooden blocks; finished with mother-of-pearl buttons.

Sizing & Fit Guide

Sizes XS to XXL. Straight notched-neck kurta (45-inch length) with elasticated comfort-fit ankle pants.

Care Instructions

Wash separately in cold water for the first two washes as natural indigo dyes may release mild excess pigment. Line dry in shade.
