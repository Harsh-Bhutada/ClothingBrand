Chikankari Festive Anarkali Kurti

Graceful and understated, the Chikankari Festive Anarkali Kurti features delicate tonal threadwork inspired by heritage Lucknowi floral motifs. Designed with a flattering fitted bodice, three-quarter sleeves, and a softly flared skirt that transitions effortlessly from daytime pujas to evening gatherings.

Fabric & Craftsmanship

Breathable Chanderi cotton-silk outer with pure mulmul lining. Hand-guided chikankari stitches accented with subtle mukaish highlights.

Sizing & Fit Guide

Sizes XS to XXL. Kurti length: 50 inches. Tailored empire waistline with flared panels that drape comfortably without clinging.

Care Instructions

Gentle hand wash separately in cold water with mild detergent, or dry clean to preserve delicate threadwork. Dry in shade.
