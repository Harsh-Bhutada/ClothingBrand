Pearl Embroidered Sharara Suit

Effortless festive movement meets delicate artistry in our Pearl Embroidered Sharara Suit. Featuring a short straight kurti embellished with tonal resham and faux-pearl motifs, wide-flared pleated sharara trousers, and a lightweight bordered dupatta.

Inquire for Price & Sizes (WhatsApp)
Fabric & Craftsmanship

Pure viscose georgette kurti and sharara lined with soft shantoon; hand-tacked pearl, sequins, and zari thread embroidery along the neckline and hem.

Sizing & Fit Guide

Sizes XS to XXL. Relaxed A-line kurti fit with half-elasticated sharara waistband and generous flare for comfortable festive wear.

Care Instructions

Dry clean only. Steam iron on low heat from the reverse side, avoiding direct contact with pearl embellishments.
