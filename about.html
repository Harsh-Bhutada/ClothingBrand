Our Story

Weaving Heritage Into Modern Wardrobes

ZARAFA brings together age-old Indian embroidery, breathable handloom fabrics, and flattering contemporary cuts across kurtis, ghagras, and shararas.

The ZARAFA Ethos

Designed For Grace & Ease

ZARAFA began with a clear vision: to create women's ethnic and everyday wear that feels just as effortless at an intimate family dinner as it does at a grand celebration.

Every collection balances refined aesthetics with practical comfort—thoughtful seam finishes, soft breathable linings, and balanced proportions that move gracefully with you from morning to evening.

Fabrics & Artisans

Natural Weaves & Hand Embroidery

We partner with regional weavers and embroidery clusters to source airy mulmul cottons, pure linen blends, luminous Chanderi silks, and delicate organzas.

From intricate chikankari and hand-block motifs on everyday kurtis to fine gota patti and zari borders on festive ghagras and sharara sets, each detail is finished with care and longevity in mind.

Wearability & Fit

Silhouettes For Every Occasion

Whether you are curating a festive trousseau, refreshing your work-ready kurti rotation, or selecting a structured Indo-Western co-ord, our racks are curated across inclusive sizes and versatile separates.

Pair our organza dupattas, embroidered juttis, and zardozi potlis effortlessly across outfits to make every look distinctly yours.
