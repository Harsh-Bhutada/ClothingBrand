Our Heritage

Rooted in Craft, Styled For Today

ZARAFA celebrates the richness of Indian textiles through modern, wearable silhouettes. Our collections bring together timeless craft traditions—delicate chikankari, luminous zari weaves, and airy handloom cottons—designed for the contemporary woman's wardrobe.

From celebratory wedding soirées in sweeping ghagras and shararas to sunlit afternoons in effortless kurtis, every garment is thoughtfully finished for flattering drape, all-day comfort, and enduring beauty.
